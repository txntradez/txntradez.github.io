About

The Person
Behind It

ICT Trader CS Grad 2025 Team USA Swimmer Futures

I started learning ICT in September 2023 and that's all I've ever studied. Never jumped around to different strategies, just locked in on ICT concepts and applied them to the futures market.

The indicators honestly came out of frustration. I was spending so much time manually plotting everything, sessions, gaps, fair value gaps, that it was taking away from actually trading. I'm a CS grad, been coding since 2021, so I just built what I needed. Then realized other traders were probably dealing with the same thing.

Growing up I swam competitively for Team USA club. Early mornings, same drills every day, grinding even when you don't feel like it, that's just how I'm wired. Trading hit me the same way. It's a challenge and I don't back down from those.

Big thank you to Michael J. Huddleston for everything he has put out freely. These scripts were built with one goal in mind, helping you see the market through time first, then price. That perspective alone can change how any trader reads the market, whether you use ICT or not. I've put serious work into these and I genuinely believe in what they do. Now it's your turn to see the market and build your edge.
