Session+

@txntradez · Invite Only

Session+ maps every major trading session directly on your chart so you always know where you are in time. Seven sessions, key institutional windows, and the levels that matter — all plotted automatically.

  • Tracks 7 distinct trading sessions including Asia, London, and New York with automatic high, low, and 50% equilibrium levels
  • Highlights Silver Bullet windows during London, NY AM, and NY PM for identifying peak institutional order flow periods
  • Maps Buy Side and Sell Side liquidity levels so you always know where stop clusters are resting
  • Plots the Opening Range Gap between prior session close and market open with premium and discount bias detection
  • Displays key institutional reference price levels that act as magnets for price reactions throughout the session

NWOG / NDOG+

@txntradez · Invite Only

NWOG/NDOG+ automatically tracks New Day and New Week Opening Gaps — the price inefficiencies left behind when markets close and reopen. It shows you where those gaps sit, how price is interacting with them, and which ones are still unfilled.

  • Automatically detects and plots New Week and New Day Opening Gaps across daily and weekly transitions
  • Classifies each gap as Premium or Discount with built in bullish and bearish fill bias so you always know what price is likely targeting
  • Tracks real time fill percentage so you can monitor how price is interacting with each gap as it develops
  • Displays internal 50% equilibrium, 25% and 75% fibonacci levels within each gap for identifying precise reaction zones
  • Historical analytics track interaction count, fill rate, and reaction strength across past gaps so you can study recurring patterns

PFVG+

@txntradez · Invite Only

PFVG+ tracks the first Fair Value Gap that forms inside each trading session and marks it as your reference zone for that period. Every session gets its own gap — so you always have a clean level to work from.

  • Tracks the first Fair Value Gap that forms within each major trading session including Asia, London, Pre Market, NY AM, NY Lunch, NY PM, and Midnight, giving you a session specific reference zone for the day
  • Classifies each gap as Bullish (BISI) or Bearish (SIBI) and displays internal 50% equilibrium, 25% and 75% fibonacci levels for identifying precise interaction zones within the gap
  • Volume Imbalance Extension refines gap boundaries using candle body separation rather than just wicks, creating more accurate zones that reflect true price imbalances
  • Dynamic highlighting automatically boosts gap visibility in real time when price enters a zone so you never miss an active interaction
  • Status table tracks current and previous gap interactions across all sessions with time elapsed so you always know which session gaps price is engaging with

I started learning ICT in September 2023 and that's all I've ever studied. Never jumped around to different strategies, just locked in on ICT concepts and applied them to the futures market.

The indicators honestly came out of frustration. I was spending so much time manually plotting everything, sessions, gaps, fair value gaps, that it was taking away from actually trading. I'm a CS grad, been coding since 2021, so I just built what I needed. Then realized other traders were probably dealing with the same thing.

Growing up I swam competitively for Team USA club. Early mornings, same drills every day, grinding even when you don't feel like it, that's just how I'm wired. Trading hit me the same way. It's a challenge and I don't back down from those.

Big thank you to Michael J. Huddleston for everything he has put out freely. These scripts were built with one goal in mind, helping you see the market through time first, then price. That perspective alone can change how any trader reads the market, whether you use ICT or not. I've put serious work into these and I genuinely believe in what they do.
